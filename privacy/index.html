Roomee

Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Roomee is an app for people who live together to split expenses, rotate chores and run polls. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Roomee app on Android or iPhone, why, and what you can do about it. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text.
  • What you add to your room: the room name, expenses (amount, category, note, date, who paid and how it's split), settlements, chores and who did them, polls and votes, and your away dates.
  • Notifications: the in-app notifications we create for you, your notification settings, and a push token for your phone so we can send it notifications. The push token identifies the app on your device, not you.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and to fix problems.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, payment card details or any data from other apps. Roomee only records amounts; no money moves through the app.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in, including emailing you a 6-digit code to confirm your email or reset your password.
  • To show your room's expenses, balances, chores and polls to you and your roommates.
  • To send notifications you've turned on, like a new expense, your chore turn or a poll closing.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Who can see it

People in your room see your name, email address, the expenses, chores and polls in the room, and whether you're away. Nobody outside your room can see any of it.

We use these service providers to run Roomee, and they only process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Data is stored on servers in Japan (Tokyo).
  • Expo, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iPhone) deliver push notifications. They receive your push token and the notification text.
  • Google (Gmail) sends our sign-in and password-reset emails.

We'll only share data otherwise if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your login, email address, name, notifications, settings and push tokens are deleted. Expenses, settlements, chore history and votes you were part of stay in your room so your roommates' balances still add up, but they show "Former roommate" instead of your name. When the last person leaves a room, the room and everything in it is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. Or use our account deletion page. If you can't sign in, email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Your rights

You can see and change your name in the app at any time, and delete your account whenever you like. You can also email us to get a copy of your data, correct it, or ask a question about how we handle it, and we'll reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR), you may have further rights, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each person can only read the rooms they belong to.

Children

Roomee isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top. For big changes we'll also tell you in the app.
